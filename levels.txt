Duck Game levels
================

Each level lists its theme, how to get its hidden pink gem, and the hint the
game shows in the inventory overlay (press i). Hints come from GEM_HINTS in
duckgame.py. Levels 9 and 11 to 14 have no gem, so they have no hint.


Level 1: Tutorial
  Theme:     A one-screen meadow with no enemies and tips painted on the backdrop.
  Gem:       Sit (Down) on the springy patch of grass in the middle for about
             1.2 seconds. She falls into a hidden cavern; climb the stepped
             ledges on the right to the gem.
  Hint:      "Lay down, down and rest where the grass is most comfy."

Level 2: Stunning Mites
  Theme:     Two small hills of stairs patrolled by a few mites; quacks stun them.
  Gem:       Hold Space on the top ledge of either hill until the full chorus
             of quacks appears. A hidden ledge appears above hill 1 and hill 2
             builds another above it. Climb back to hill 1 and take the gem
             from the upper ledge.
  Hint:      "Shout from the top of each hill as long as your breath will last,
             then climb as high as the echoes."

Level 3: Mites
  Theme:     A long scrolling level of stair hills full of mites, ending at the
             pea-shooter on the far-right platform.
  Gem:       Have 6 mites stunned at the same moment (quack when 6 are within
             range). A zigzag of five ledges appears above the top ledge of
             hill 2, leading up to the gem.
  Hint:      "When enough little ones have been put to sleep, a stair will rise
             above the second hill."

Level 4: Three Hills
  Theme:     Three hills side by side: mites on hill 1, a shooting lesson on
             hill 2, and beetles on hill 3.
  Gem:       Leap of faith from the left end of the top ledge of the third hill
             (its left end glows faintly pink while you stand there). Invisible
             ledges appear as you land on them; the gem shows after the 4th.
  Hint:      "Not every step can be seen; take a leap of faith where the left
             edge gleams."

Level 5: Moving Ledges
  Theme:     Sliding ledges carry the duck to the pea-shooter and across a wide
             gap to a staircase of beetles.
  Gem:       Ride the beetle's high sliding ledge and jump from its left end
             (it glows faintly pink there). A chain of hidden ledges appears
             above it, leading to the gem.
  Hint:      "Ride with the beetle's neighbour, then leap from where it begins."

Level 6: Mushrooms
  Theme:     A tall climb up stairs, mushrooms and sliding ledges, with mites,
             beetles and wind gusts near the top.
  Gem:       Walk to the right end of the summit ledge. A new mushroom sprouts
             that looks like the others. Bounce on it while holding Up for the
             bigger bounce to reach the gem. Beware: the level ends 3 seconds
             after the last achievement.
  Hint:      "The top is not the end. Go right and the ground will answer."

Level 7: Medium Flood
  Theme:     A medium tower of staggered ledges with water that rises steadily.
  Gem:       Drown while standing on the grass. A mermaid revives you in a
             cavern; after a moment a hidden room opens in the rock and you
             can collect the gem. Climb the stairs back to the grass and the
             flood resumes.
  Hint:      "Sometimes the deep is kind. Take a last breath where the grass ends."

Level 8: Final Climb
  Theme:     Level 6 mirrored, with mushrooms, sliding ledges, six beetles, wind
             and rising water.
  Gem:       Walk off the right end of a ledge about 50 rows up (ledges 24 and
             26 work) and land on a lone ledge far to the right of the tower.
             Jump from its right end (it glows faintly) to reveal a chain of
             hidden ledges; the gem shows after the 4th.
  Hint:      "Step off the edge, far from the climb, and a hidden ledge will
             catch you."

Level 9: Bramble Hill
  Theme:     A tall grassy zigzag of 21 ledges with two mushrooms and a
             sliding ledge, patrolled by mites, beetles, hedgehogs and
             armadillos. Hedgehogs walk uphill, curl into a
             spiky ball at the top and roll back down; peas bounce off the
             ball, so wait for it to uncurl. Armadillos can only be hurt by a
             pea in the back; one that hits the front bounces back and hurts
             the duck (sit down or jump to get under it).
  Gem:       None.
  Hint:      None.

Level 10: Flood Tower
  Theme:     A tall tower of ledges; the water rises, then speeds up in the rain.
  Gem:       When the rain starts (reach ledge 12), a storm cloud appears as a
             sliding ledge. Ride it to the end where the gem hangs. The first
             landing lowers the water by 6 rows, and it holds still while you
             stand on the cloud.
  Hint:      "When the sky weeps, something floats that is meant to be ridden."

Level 11: Ice Rink
  Theme:     An icy rink: the duck slides instead of walking. Beetles, a
             pea-shooter and a climb to the top.
  Gem:       None.
  Hint:      None.

Level 12: Hard Ice Rink
  Theme:     A harder rink with narrower ledges, a sliding ledge and a mushroom.
  Gem:       None.
  Hint:      None.

Level 13: Grand Tour
  Theme:     A wide, tall world: ice, a sliding ledge, mushrooms, a 60-column
             chasm crossed with the umbrella glide, and a windy tower with 14
             beetles.
  Gem:       None.
  Hint:      None.

Level 14: The Full Game
  Theme:     A 2400-column world with lakes, caves, pearls, hunger, hats, eggs,
             chicks, predators and 17 achievements.
  Gem:       None.
  Hint:      None.
