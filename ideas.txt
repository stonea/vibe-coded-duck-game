Wild level ideas for the duck game
(Flood Tower is being built as level 5, so it is not listed here.)

1. Gravity Flip Cavern: Stepping on glowing pads flips gravity, so the duck walks on the ceiling. Coins and ledges are placed on both sides.
2. Ice Rink: Low friction, so the duck slides and has to brake. Pea-shooter recoil pushes her backwards, so shots can steer her across gaps.
3. Night Level: Only a small circle around the duck is visible. Fireflies reveal hidden coins, and bats hide in the dark.
4. Conveyor Factory: Ledges are belts moving in alternating directions. You time jumps while beetles ride along.
5. Boss Beetle: A giant beetle arena with several phases. It charges, shoots shockwaves and spawns minions, and you have to use the platforms.
6. Cloud Hop: Clouds drift and some dissolve after about a second. Thunder gusts shove you around.
7. Egg Heist: A fox patrols and steals your egg. Hide it in nests, lure the fox away and sneak back.
8. Teleport Maze: Paired portals, with a few decoys that drop you in a lake. Find the real route to the summit.
9. Time Loop: Each death replays your previous run as a ghost duck. Ghosts can press switches, so you cooperate with yourself.
10. Wind Tunnel Sprint: A horizontal auto-scrolling level with constant gusts. Jump between moving platforms, and umbrella gliding is mandatory.
11. Bouncy Castle: The whole floor is mushrooms with variable bounce strength. Bounce chains unlock a high secret ledge.
12. Pond Puzzle: Move floating logs and lily pads to build a bridge. Swimming is slow and dangerous.
13. Rhythm Level: Platforms appear and disappear on a beat, and the quack key is a beat-synced action.
14. Giant Bread Bakery: The duck is tiny inside a gigantic kitchen. Rising dough platforms swell and collapse, and rolling pins sweep across the floor. Melted butter pools are slippery.
15. Shrink-and-Grow Garden: Magic berries make the duck tiny or huge. Tiny fits through mouse holes, huge smashes crates and stomps beetles, and timers force you to switch at the right moment.
16. Upside-Down Waterfall: Water flows upward in streams you can ride like elevators. Rocks fall up too, so you dodge them overhead while climbing.
17. Moon Duck: Low gravity with floaty giant jumps. Moon rocks drift in orbit, and the pea-shooter is a big recoil rocket booster.
18. Haunted Pond Mansion: Ghost ducklings follow you and turn into platforms when you quack. Doors lead to randomly swapped rooms, and a lantern reveals invisible bridges.
19. Pinball Pond: The level is a giant pinball table. Bumpers launch you, flippers on the sides are controlled with left and right, and coins score multipliers.
20. Shadow Puppet Level: The duck walks on a wall lit by a lamp. Moving the lamp changes the shadows of objects, and shadows become the platforms.
21. Magnet Mines: Magnetic coins and metal crates pull the duck. Polarity switches flip attraction to repulsion, so you swing across gaps on magnetic rails.
22. Snowball Avalanche: Roll a snowball downhill so it grows and crushes obstacles. Steer it to build a ramp, then jump off before the cliff.
23. Dream Level: Rooms rearrange whenever you blink (every few seconds). Stairs loop, the exit moves, and the quack key wakes you into a different layout.
24. Swapping Duckling Relay: Control three ducklings with different powers (flap high, swim fast, shoot). Swap between them to carry a lost egg through to the nest.
25. Volcano Escape: Lava rises while platforms crumble. Geysers launch you upward, and cooling lava with water pistols makes temporary stepping stones.
26. Pirate Ship Storm: A rocking deck tilts the whole screen's gravity direction. Barrels roll, cannons fire at you, and kraken tentacles grab platforms.
27. Gravity Well Planets: Tiny planetoids each have their own gravity. Jump between them, orbiting around to reach distant coins.
28. Mirror World: The screen shows two ducks, one normal and one mirrored. Both move together, and each must reach its own exit while obstacles differ in each half.
29. Fox Chase Treadmill: A fox chases you through an endless looping level. Each lap, the layout mutates, and you must grab coins to unlock the exit.
30. Cooking Contest: Collect ingredients across platforms in recipe order. Wrong order spawns angry soup monsters, and the final stew is the boss fight.

Enemy ideas for the duck game

1. Bramble Hedgehog: Curls into a spiky ball and rolls down slopes. Peas bounce off the spines, so you have to wait for it to uncurl, or bounce it into a pit.
2. Snapping Turtle: Slow and armoured. Peas only hurt when it pokes its head out to snap, and its shell can be used as a floating platform once it is stunned.
3. Slime Slug: Leaves a sticky trail that slows the duck. A quack makes it shrink and speed up, and salt crumbs (a new pickup) dissolve it.
4. Wasp Swarm: A cloud of tiny wasps that hovers over a nest and chases the duck once she gets close. One quack scatters it for a few seconds, and fleeing into water loses it.
5. Dragonfly Dive-Bomber: Hovers high above, telegraphs with a glint, then dives in a straight line. Dodge, then shoot it while it recovers on the ground.
6. Jumping Frog Hopper: Hops in big arcs and lands on ledges with a shockwave. It can be stomped from above, but its tongue pulls the duck toward it.
7. Snake in the Reeds: Hides in grass and strikes when the duck walks past. Sitting (Down) makes the duck hard to see, and rustling grass gives it away.
8. Raccoon Thief: Steals a coin or pea pack and runs off. Shoot or chase it down to get the item back, and it gets faster with each theft.
9. Crow Egg-Snatcher: Circles over the nest and swoops to grab eggs and chicks. A loud quack scares it away, and a scarecrow decoy keeps it busy.
10. Pike Fish: Lurks in the deep part of lakes and lunges at a diving duck. It is attracted to splashing, so floating slowly keeps it away. Pearls guard its cave.
11. Electric Eel: Charges up and zaps the water around it. Standing on a ledge above a lake is safe, but swimming through while it recharges is not.
12. Jellyfish Drifter: Floats slowly through lakes. The sting slows the duck, and the dome can be bounced on like a mushroom.
13. Mole Digger: Tunnels under the ground and pops up under the duck. Shows a mound before surfacing, and stays stunned if it comes up under a ledge.
14. Ant Column: A line of ants marching along ledges. Each ant is weak but they hurt in a chain, and a pea that hits the lead ant makes the rest turn around.
15. Spider Dropper: Hangs from a silk thread on the ceiling, then drops when the duck passes below. Shoot the thread to drop it early, and the webs left behind make sticky platforms.
16. Stag Beetle Brute: A bigger beetle with antlers. It charges, and its antlers lock into walls so it can be stuck for a few seconds. Needs a hit from behind.
17. Firefly Decoy: Looks like a harmless coin-glow in the dark levels, but leads the duck over a gap or into a bat. Gets the true coin-glow only when it blinks twice.
18. Owl Sentinel: Sits on a high branch and swivels. When it spots the duck it hoots and calls other enemies. Stay behind cover or knock it off with a pea.
19. Ice Imp: Lives on the ice levels and slides at high speed. Its path cannot be steered, so you step out of the way and let it crash into walls.
20. Fire Ember Sprite: Lava level only. Hops out of the lava and leaves burning platforms behind. Water pistol shots put it out and turn it into a stepping stone.
21. Wind Wisp: A swirl that pushes the duck sideways. Umbrella gliding makes it worse, and shooting it turns it into a tailwind for a short time.
22. Magnet Crab: Walks sideways, pulls metal crates and coins toward itself. Flip its polarity with a quack to launch the crates at other enemies.
23. Mimic Chest: Looks like a pea pack or a coin. Bites when picked up, but defeating it drops a better reward.
24. Bubble Blower: Floats around and traps the duck in a bubble that carries her upward and away. Jump out by popping the bubble with a pea.
25. Shadow Duck: A dark copy that copies the duck's last run, one second behind. Dies to a quack when facing it, and leads you toward hidden coins.
26. Mini Boss: Mama Beetle: Guards a nest of baby beetles. Peas hurt her, but each wave of babies spawns more beetles, so the trick is to break the eggs first.
27. Mini Boss: Giant Pike: Rises out of the lake in a flood level. Dodging its jumps with the umbrella glide lets you shoot its weak spot.
28. Mini Boss: The Fox King: Level 13 finale. Summons foxes, steals the golden egg, and has to be herded into the pond where it loses its crown.
